Privacy Policy

Workouts: Gym Planner collects no data. Effective 7 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no server and no sign-up. We do not receive, store or share any information about you.

What the app does not do

  • No tracking and no advertising.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • The app itself makes no network connections. If you choose iCloud Drive backup, iOS syncs those backup files to your own iCloud account.

Where your data lives

Your weeks, days, exercises and notes are stored on your iPhone only.

Backups

The app can write backup files of your plan. Automatic backups are saved on your iPhone, in the Files app under On My iPhone › Workouts, or, if you choose it in Settings, in your own iCloud Drive under Workouts. You can also export a backup file yourself. These files are yours: you decide where they go and who gets them. We never see them.

If you have iCloud Backup turned on for your iPhone, Apple's standard device backup may include the app's data, and that is handled by Apple under your own Apple account and its terms.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either. It has no accounts, ads or social features.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

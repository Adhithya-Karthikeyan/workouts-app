Workouts: Gym Planner

A free, offline gym workout planner for iPhone. Plan your weeks, days and supersets, then read the plan at the gym.

Workouts is a planner, not a tracker. It needs no account and no internet connection, and it has no ads or tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Nothing you plan is sent anywhere.

How do I back up my plan?

Automatic Backup is on by default. After every change the app saves a backup file you can open in the Files app under On My iPhone › Workouts › Backups. It keeps the newest 10.

To make a copy yourself, open the Settings tab and tap Export Backup. Save the file to Files, iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself.

How do I restore a backup?

Open the Settings tab and tap Import Backup, then choose a backup file. The app asks whether to Merge it into what you have or Replace All. Replace All deletes your current weeks first, so export a backup before using it.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

  1. On the old phone, open Settings and tap Export Backup.
  2. Send the file to the new phone with AirDrop, iCloud Drive or email.
  3. Install Workouts on the new phone, open Settings and tap Import Backup.

You can also restore from an iCloud or computer backup of your old iPhone if you have one.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no purchases, subscriptions or ads.

Does it need internet?

No. Workouts works fully offline.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhone running iOS 27 or later. Weights are shown in kilograms (kg).
